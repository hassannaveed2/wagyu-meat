Prologue

In Japan, wagyu is not a product. It is thirty months of patience, a family’s name and a single season, kept for one evening at your table.

We bring that tradition to Pakistan with nothing lost on the way: halal from farm to box, graded and traceable, and kept cold from Japan to your door.

匠The Craft

Three disciplines, one cut.

Before a single slice reaches you, three crafts have already done their quiet work.

一Origin · 産地

Raised slowly, in the mist.

Kuroge cattle graze the hills of Kagoshima, Miyazaki and Hokkaido for more than thirty months. Calm animals, clean water, a patient diet: marbling cannot be hurried.

Kagoshima · Miyazaki · Hokkaido

二Grade · 格付

Graded, never guessed.

Every A5 is graded by the Japan Meat Grading Association and carries a ten-digit cattle ID. Its certificate travels with it, so you can trace the farm yourself.

A5 · BMS 9–12 · JMGA

三The cut · 包丁

With a single-bevel yanagiba, each block is portioned into steaks, yakiniku and shabu-shabu slices, then vacuum-sealed and frozen at its peak.

作法The Ritual

Three gestures at the table.

  1. 壱

    Thaw解凍

    The night before, move it from freezer to fridge. Patience, once again.

  2. 弐

    Sear焼く

    A very hot pan or binchotan. Salt at the last moment. Two minutes a side.

  3. 参

    Rest休ませる

    Five minutes of rest, then slice against the grain. Serve small, savour slowly.
